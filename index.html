Second Brain

Second Brain is a private, single-user tool built and used by Michael Bonera. It connects his own Google accounts (Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive) to his personal note archive, so that he can triage his email, keep his calendar in sync with his notes and read his own files.

The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Strumento personale a uso esclusivo di Michael Bonera: collega i suoi account Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) al suo archivio di note.
